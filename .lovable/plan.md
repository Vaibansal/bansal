

# Vaibhav Bansal — Personal Portfolio Website

## Design Direction
Light & elegant theme with cream/white backgrounds, refined serif + sans-serif typography, warm accent colors, and rich scroll-triggered animations (fade-ins, slide-ups, staggered reveals).

## Pages & Sections (Single-Page Layout)

### 1. **Hero Section**
- Large heading: "Vaibhav Bansal" with an animated tagline like *"Product Leader · Founder · Builder"*
- Smooth text reveal animation on load
- CTA buttons: "View My Work" and "Get In Touch"
- Subtle animated background pattern or gradient accent

### 2. **About Me**
- Brief bio highlighting the "jack of all trades" persona — product management, entrepreneurship, engineering background
- Key stats animated on scroll (e.g., "$17M+ ARR driven", "500+ team led", "IIT Roorkee")

### 3. **Experience Timeline**
- Vertical animated timeline showing career progression:
  - **UnifyApps** — Senior Product Manager
  - **Sprinklr** — PM → Senior Product Analyst → APM
- Each entry slides in on scroll with role, company, dates, and key highlights

### 4. **Projects**
- Featured card for **TBS PLANET Comics** (founder story)
- Clean card layout with hover animations and key metrics
- Room to add more projects later

### 5. **Skills**
- Two categories: Technical (SQL, Figma, Postman, Tableau) and Soft Skills (Product Design, Stakeholder Management, GTM Strategy, etc.)
- Animated skill badges/tags that stagger in on scroll

### 6. **Achievements**
- Highlight cards for competition wins and IIT-JEE rank
- Subtle scale-in animations

### 7. **Contact / Footer**
- Contact form or direct links (email, phone)
- Social links section
- Clean minimal footer

## Navigation
- Sticky top navbar with smooth-scroll links to each section
- Animated underline on hover for nav items
- Mobile hamburger menu

## Animations
- Intersection Observer-based scroll animations (fade-in, slide-up, stagger)
- Smooth parallax-like effects on hero
- Hover effects on cards and buttons
- Counter animations for stats

## Data
- All resume content will be hardcoded from the provided resume — easily editable later
