What It DoesSix jobs, one pass
01

Reads and understands it

Drop in a brief — pasted text, a document, even a scanned PDF or image. Every relevant field is extracted into your master metadata schema, with a confidence level and the exact source snippet behind each value. It never invents cast, dates, or figures — if something isn't there, it says so.

02

Knows what's missing

Objectively checks the brief against your required-vs-nice-to-have field list and produces a ready-to-send request sheet you can email straight back to the production house.

03

Scores the data

Every submission gets a 0–100 data-richness score — at a glance you see what's catalogue-ready and what needs work.

04

Assigns a content rating

An age certification (U / U-A 7+ / 13+ / 16+ / A) with advisory flags and a written rationale — calibrated against your own past rating decisions, so it matches your network's bar, not a generic one.

05

Writes the marketing

An on-air hook, three tagline options, comparable titles, a recommended slot, and SEO keywords — for every brief.

06

Routes it to a human

Everything lands in a review screen where a person approves, edits, or sends it back. Nothing auto-publishes when the stakes are high.

FAQ

What content-ops leads ask first.

Will it invent metadata when the brief is vague?+

No — this is the core design principle. Every field carries confidence and source evidence. If it isn't in the brief, the field stays empty and lands on the follow-up sheet.

Can we trust the age ratings?+

Ratings are calibrated against your own past rating decisions, not a generic standard. Each rating carries advisory flags and a written rationale, and any uncertain call is escalated to a human reviewer before it's finalized.

What formats can it read?+

Pasted text, documents, spreadsheets, scanned PDFs, and images. OCR runs at ingestion, so even a photographed or forwarded brief is normalized into the same structured record.

Does it replace our content-ops team?+

No — it removes the mechanical first pass. Your team stops re-typing metadata and instead reviews, approves, or corrects a ready-made record. Every human change is captured in the audit trail and feeds back into the calibration.
